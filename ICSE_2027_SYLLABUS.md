# ICSE Class X Syllabus - Year 2027

## 1. SECOND LANGUAGE - ODIA (Code: 07)
There will be one paper of three hours duration carrying 80 marks and Internal Assessment of 20 marks.

### Section A: Language (40 Marks)
1. **Composition**: Candidates will be required to write one composition (essay/narrative/descriptive), in Odia (about 250 words). Choice of subjects will be given.
2. **Letter Writing**: Formal or informal letter in Odia. The layout of the letter with address, introduction, conclusion, etc., will be assessed.
3. **Comprehension**: An unseen passage of about 250 words in Odia. Questions on the passage will be set for answers in Odia to test understanding.
4. **Grammar**: Tests in the use of vocabulary, syntax, idioms, synthesis of sentences, and formation of sentences embodying given words/forms. Does not require knowledge of grammatical terms.

### Section B: Prescribed Texts (40 Marks)
Candidates will be required to answer four questions from ONLY two of the prescribed textbooks.
- **Prose / Short Stories**: Detailed study of prescribed stories (e.g., Rebati, Dhauli, etc., as per the official appendix).
- **Poetry**: Detailed study of prescribed poems.
- **Drama/Novel**: If opted, study of the prescribed long-form text.

## 2. ECONOMICS (Code: 64)
There will be one paper of two hours duration carrying 80 marks and Internal Assessment of 20 marks.

### 1. The Productive Mechanism
- Factors of production: Land, Labour, Capital, and Entrepreneur.
- Land: meaning and characteristics.
- Labour: meaning, characteristics, and division of labour.
- Capital: meaning, characteristics, and capital formation.
- Entrepreneur: meaning, functions, and role.

### 2. Theory of Demand and Supply
- Meaning and Law of Demand.
- Exceptions to the Law of Demand.
- Meaning and Law of Supply.
- Equilibrium Price: Interaction of Demand and Supply.

### 3. Market
- Meaning and types of markets.
- Perfect Competition and Monopoly: basic concepts and features.
- Monopolistic Competition: basic concepts and features.

### 4. Banking in India
- Commercial Banks: meaning and functions.
- Central Bank (RBI): meaning and functions.
- Difference between Commercial Banks and the Central Bank.

### 5. Inflation
- Meaning and types of inflation (creeping, walking, running, hyper).
- Causes of inflation (Demand-pull and Cost-push).
- Effects of inflation on different sections of society.

### 6. Public Finance
- Public Revenue: meaning and sources (Tax and Non-tax).
- Tax: Direct and Indirect Taxes (meaning, merits, demerits).
- Public Expenditure: meaning and types.
- Public Debt: meaning.

### 7. Consumer Awareness
- Meaning of Consumer Exploitation.
- Rights and Duties of Consumers.
- Food adulteration.
- Consumer Protection Act (COPRA).
