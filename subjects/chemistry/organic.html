Organic Structure & IUPAC Naming

(•‿•)
Loading Chem-Bot...

1. Unique Nature of Carbon

  • Tetravalency: Carbon has a valency of 4 and can share 4 electrons to form 4 covalent bonds.
  • Catenation: The unique property of self-linking among carbon atoms to form straight, branched, or cyclic molecular chains.

2. Homologous Series

A group of organic compounds having the same functional group, similar chemical properties, and showing a gradation in physical properties, where successive members differ by a **$-CH_2-$ group** and **$14\text{ a.m.u.}$** in molecular weight.

3. Structural Isomerism

Compounds having the **same molecular formula but different structural arrangements** of carbon atoms.

  • Chain Isomerism: Difference in carbon chain layout (e.g. Butane has $n$-butane and isobutane).
  • Position Isomerism: Difference in the position of functional groups or multiple bonds (e.g. But-1-ene and But-2-ene).

4. Basic IUPAC Rules

  1. Identify the longest carbon chain (Word root: Meth-, Eth-, Prop-, But-, Pent-).
  2. Identify primary suffix (Alkane: -ane, Alkene: -ene, Alkyne: -yne).
  3. Identify functional groups / secondary suffix: Alcohols ($R-OH$, **-ol**), Aldehydes ($R-CHO$, **-al**), Carboxylic Acids ($R-COOH$, **-oic acid**).
  4. Number the chain starting from the end closest to the functional group / double bond.

Hydrocarbons, Ethanol & Ethanoic Acid

(•‿•)
Loading Chem-Bot...

1. Hydrocarbon Reactions

  • Alkanes (Saturated): Undergo **Substitution Reactions** (one or more H atoms replaced).
    Chlorination: $CH_4 + Cl_2 \xrightarrow{\text{diffuse sunlight}} CH_3Cl + HCl$.
  • Alkenes & Alkynes (Unsaturated): Undergo **Addition Reactions** (multiple bonds break to add elements).
    Bromine Water Test: Addition of reddish-brown bromine water to Ethene/Ethyne decolorizes the bromine water, proving unsaturation ($CH_2=CH_2 + Br_2 \rightarrow CH_2Br-CH_2Br$). Saturated alkanes do not react under similar conditions.

2. Ethanol ($C_2H_5OH$)

  • Dehydration: Heating ethanol with excess concentrated $H_2SO_4$ at $170^\circ\text{C}$ yields Ethene gas:
    $C_2H_5OH \xrightarrow{\text{conc } H_2SO_4, 170^\circ\text{C}} CH_2=CH_2 + H_2O$.
  • Oxidation: Oxidized to Ethanoic acid using alkaline $KMnO_4$ or acidic $K_2Cr_2O_7$.

3. Ethanoic Acid ($CH_3COOH$)

  • Esterification: Reacts with Ethanol under warm concentrated $H_2SO_4$ catalyst to yield sweet-smelling **Ethyl Acetate (Ester)**:
    $CH_3COOH + C_2H_5OH \xrightarrow{\text{conc } H_2SO_4} CH_3COOC_2H_5 + H_2O$.
