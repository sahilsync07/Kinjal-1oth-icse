1. Hydrogen Chloride ($HCl$)

  • Lab Preparation: $NaCl + H_2SO_4 \xrightarrow{<200^\circ\text{C}} NaHSO_4 + HCl\uparrow$. (Conc $H_2SO_4$ is used as a non-volatile acid to displace volatile $HCl$). Collected by upward displacement of air (denser than air). Dried by Concentrated Sulfuric Acid.
  • Fountain Experiment: Demonstrates high solubility of $HCl$ in water. Water dissolves $HCl$ gas in a flask, creating a partial vacuum. Atmospheric pressure forces water up a tube, spraying a red fountain (due to acidic litmus shift).
  • Important Reaction: $HCl(g) + NH_3(g) \rightarrow NH_4Cl(s)$ (dense white fumes — two gases forming a solid).

2. Ammonia ($NH_3$)

  • Lab Preparation: $2NH_4Cl + Ca(OH)_2 \xrightarrow{\Delta} CaCl_2 + 2H_2O + 2NH_3\uparrow$. Dried **only** using Quicklime ($CaO$). Other drying agents like $P_2O_5$ or conc $H_2SO_4$ react chemically with basic Ammonia. Collected by downward displacement of air (lighter than air).
  • Fountain Experiment: Shows high solubility. Forms a **blue fountain** (due to alkaline litmus shift).
  • Haber's Process (Industrial): $N_2 + 3H_2 \rightleftharpoons 2NH_3 + \text{heat}$.
    Conditions: $450-500^\circ\text{C}$, $200-900\text{ atm}$, Finely divided **Iron** catalyst, **Molybdenum** promoter.

Study of Nitric & Sulfuric Acids

(•‿•)
Loading Chem-Bot...

1. Nitric Acid ($HNO_3$)

  • Lab Preparation: $KNO_3 + H_2SO_4 \xrightarrow{<200^\circ\text{C}} KHSO_4 + HNO_3$. Prepared in an **all-glass retort** below $200^\circ\text{C}$ to prevent acid thermal decomposition.
  • Ostwald's Process (Industrial):
    1. *Catalytic Oxidation:* $4NH_3 + 5O_2 \xrightarrow{\text{Pt, } 800^\circ\text{C}} 4NO + 6H_2O$.
    2. *Nitric Oxide Oxidation:* $2NO + O_2 \rightarrow 2NO_2$.
    3. *Absorption:* $4NO_2 + 2H_2O + O_2 \rightarrow 4HNO_3$.
  • Oxidizing Actions: Conc $HNO_3$ is a strong oxidizing agent, reacting with metals (e.g. Copper) and non-metals (e.g. Carbon) to release $NO_2$ gas.
    Equation: $C + 4HNO_3(\text{conc}) \rightarrow CO_2 + 4NO_2 + 2H_2O$.

2. Sulfuric Acid ($H_2SO_4$)

  • Contact Process (Industrial):
    1. *Sulfur oxidation:* $S + O_2 \rightarrow SO_2$.
    2. *Catalytic Conversion:* $2SO_2 + O_2 \rightleftharpoons 2SO_3$. (Catalyst: **Vanadium Pentoxide $V_2O_5$**, $450^\circ\text{C}$).
    3. *Absorption:* $SO_3$ is absorbed in conc $H_2SO_4$ to form **Oleum ($H_2S_2O_7$)**: $SO_3 + H_2SO_4 \rightarrow H_2S_2O_7$. Direct absorption in water is avoided as it forms a dense highly exothermic acid mist.
    4. *Dilution:* Oleum is diluted with calculated water: $H_2S_2O_7 + H_2O \rightarrow 2H_2SO_4$.
  • Dehydrating Agent: Removes chemically combined water from organic compounds. E.g. charring of cane sugar:
    $C_{12}H_{22}O_{11} \xrightarrow{\text{conc } H_2SO_4} 12C (\text{black spongy carbon}) + 11H_2O$.
