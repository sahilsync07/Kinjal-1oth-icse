1. The Notice Marking Scheme

The ICSE Board evaluates notices strictly on 5 specific parameters (each worth 1 Mark):

Parameter Requirement & Rules Marks
1. Creative Title Must catch attention, relate directly to the event (e.g. War of Words for debate, Paint the World for art). Avoid generic titles like "Debate Notice". 1 Mark
2. Name of the Event State exactly what the event is (e.g. Inter-School Debate Competition). Keep it professional. 1 Mark
3. Date of the Event Must be written in formal words. **Accepted**: 24th May 2027 or May 24, 2027. **Rejected**: 24/05/2027 or 24-5-27. 1 Mark
4. Time of the Event State specific hours (e.g., 9:00 AM to 1:00 PM). Avoid vague terms like "morning". 1 Mark
5. Venue (Two-Tier) Must state from specific to general: **[Small Venue], [Large Venue]** (e.g., Assembly Hall, St. Jude's School). 1 Mark

2. Target Audience Statement

Although it does not have a separate mark, the notice body must conclude with an explicit invitation to the target audience (e.g., "All students of classes IX and X who wish to participate are requested to register with...").

1. E-mail Layout Parameters

E-mail writing demands perfect, rigid formal protocols. The marks are split as follows:

  • To (Recipient Address) [1/2 Mark]: Must be a completely lowercase, valid-looking formal email address (e.g. principal@stmarysschool.com). Do not use spaces or capital letters.
  • Subject Line [1/2 Mark]: Must be formal, concise, and directly name the event (e.g., Invitation to the Inter-School Debate Competition). Do not write sentences.
  • Salutation [1/2 Mark]: Formal greeting only. Use Dear Sir, or Dear Madam,. Avoid "Respected Sir" or informal greetings like "Hello Sir".
  • Opening & Body Content [2 Marks]:
    • The opening sentence must state the purpose of the email immediately (e.g., "I am writing to invite your school to participate in...").
    • The body must include the **identical** Date, Time, and Venue details that were specified in your Notice. Any contradiction loses marks immediately!
  • Closing Sentence & Subscription [1.5 Marks]:
    • Must have a formal call-to-action closing (e.g., "We look forward to your school's active participation.") [1/2 Mark].
    • Must end with: Yours faithfully, [1/2 Mark] followed by your Name and Designation [1/2 Mark]. Note: capitalization must be exactly "Yours faithfully," (capital Y, lowercase f, followed by a comma).
