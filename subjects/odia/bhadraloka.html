ଗଳ୍ପରେ 'ଭଦ୍ରଲୋକ' ନାମରେ ଜଣେ ଚରିତ୍ର ଅଛନ୍ତି ଯିଏ ସମାଜରେ ନିଜକୁ ଭଦ୍ର ଓ ସଭ୍ୟ ବୋଲି ଦେଖାନ୍ତି। ସେ ଉଚ୍ଚ ଶ୍ରେଣୀର ଲୋକ ଓ ଗରିବ ଲୋକଙ୍କୁ ତୁଚ୍ଛ ମନେ କରନ୍ତି। ସମାଜରେ ଶ୍ରେଣୀ ବିଭାଜନ ଓ ଧନୀ-ଗରିବ ମଧ୍ୟରେ ଥିବା ବ୍ୟବଧାନ ଗଳ୍ପର ମୂଳ ଆଧାର। (The story features a character called 'Bhadraloka' who presents himself as respectable in society. He belongs to the upper class and looks down on the poor. The class divide between rich and poor forms the core of the story.)

His gentlemanliness was mere outward show. True gentility lies not in wealth but in character and humanity. The author delivers a powerful message against social hypocrisy.)
