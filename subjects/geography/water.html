Methods of Irrigation

(•‿•)
Loading Geo-Bot...

1. Wells and Tube Wells

  • Where: Northern Plains (UP, Punjab, Haryana) because the soil is soft to dig and groundwater is abundant.
  • Advantages: Cheap, independent source of water, reliable even in dry seasons.
  • Disadvantages: Tube wells require electricity. Overuse depletes the water table rapidly.

2. Canals (Inundation vs Perennial)

  • Where: UP, Punjab, Haryana, and coastal deltas.
  • Perennial Canals: Drawn from perennial rivers (snow-fed) or dams. Provide water year-round.
  • Inundation Canals: Drawn directly from rivers without dams. Only have water when the river is in flood.
  • Disadvantage: Can lead to water-logging, which brings harmful salts to the surface (salinization).

3. Tank Irrigation

  • Where: Deccan Plateau (Andhra Pradesh, Tamil Nadu, Karnataka).
  • Why there? The hard, rocky terrain makes digging wells difficult. The natural depressions in rocks easily collect rainwater.
  • Disadvantages: Tanks dry up in summer because they depend on rain. High evaporation rate due to large exposed surface area.

Rainwater Harvesting

(•‿•)
Loading Geo-Bot...

Rainwater Harvesting (RWH) is the technique of collecting and storing rainwater for future use, rather than letting it run off.

Why is RWH necessary in India?

  1. To meet the increasing demand for water due to population growth.
  2. To reduce surface runoff which causes floods and soil erosion.
  3. To raise the rapidly depleting underground water table.
  4. Because Indian rainfall is highly seasonal (occurs mostly in 3-4 months).

Methods of RWH

  • Rooftop Harvesting: Rainwater falling on roofs is piped into underground tanks or pits.
  • Johads / Check Dams: Small earthen dams built across streams in Rajasthan to capture water and allow it to percolate into the soil.
  • Recharge Trenches: Pits dug to allow water to seep into the ground, replenishing aquifers.

ICSE Give-Reason Masterclass

Scenario / QuestionGeographical Reason
Why is tank irrigation popular in South India?The Deccan plateau consists of hard, non-porous rocks where digging wells/canals is impossible. Natural rock depressions act as excellent tanks.
Why is well irrigation popular in North India?The northern plains have soft alluvial soil (easy to dig) and a high groundwater table fed by perennial rivers.
Why does canal irrigation cause salinization?Over-irrigation leads to water-logging. When the water evaporates, it leaves behind harmful alkaline salts on the surface, ruining fertility.
Why is rainwater harvesting heavily promoted in Rajasthan?Rainfall is scanty (<50cm), there are no perennial rivers, and groundwater levels are dangerously low. RWH is the only way to sustain life.
