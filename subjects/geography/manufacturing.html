Manufacturing is the process of converting raw materials into more valuable finished products on a large scale. It is considered the backbone of any economy.

Agro-based Industries

(•‿•)
Loading Geo-Bot...

1. Sugar Industry

  • Location Factors: Sugarcane is a bulky, weight-losing, and perishable crop. Sucrose content begins to dry up within 24 hours of harvest. Hence, mills must be located close to fields (raw material oriented).
  • Major Centers: Uttar Pradesh (Sub-tropical belt), Maharashtra, Tamil Nadu (Tropical belt - yields higher sucrose content due to maritime influence and longer crushing season).
  • Problems: Seasonal nature of industry, old machinery, short crushing season, high cost of transport, and underutilization of by-products like bagasse and molasses.

2. Cotton Textile Industry

  • Location Factors: Cotton is a pure raw material (does not lose weight in manufacturing). It can be transported easily. However, a humid climate is highly preferred as it prevents thread breakage during spinning.
  • Major Centers: Mumbai (known as Cottonopolis of India), Ahmedabad (Manchester of India), Coimbatore (South India hub).
  • Problems: Shortage of raw cotton (medium/long staple), sick mills with obsolete machinery, power shortages, and tough competition from synthetic fibers.

3. Jute Textile Industry

  • Location Factors: Concentrated along the Hooghly River in West Bengal because of proximity to jute fields, abundant soft water for retting, cheap coal from Raniganj, and excellent transport.
  • Problems: Loss of jute-growing tracts to Bangladesh after partition, stiff competition from synthetic packing material, and obsolete machinery.

Mineral-based & IT Industries

(•‿•)
Loading Geo-Bot...

1. Iron and Steel Industry (The Key Industry)

  • Significance: All other industries depend on it for machinery, tools, and construction. Hence, it is called a basic/key industry.
  • Location Factors: Requires bulky and weight-losing raw materials (iron ore, coking coal, limestone, manganese). Hence, plants are located near raw materials (Chhota Nagpur Plateau).
  • TISCO (Jamshedpur): Private sector, oldest plant. Located at Sakchi (Jamshedpur). Gets iron ore from Noamundi (Jharkhand), coal from Jharia (Jharkhand), water from Kharkai and Subarnarekha rivers.
  • Bhilai Steel Plant: Located in Chhattisgarh. Gets iron ore from Dalli-Rajhara, coal from Korba, water from Tandula canal.
  • Rourkela Steel Plant: Located in Odisha. Gets iron ore from Sundargarh, coal from Jharia/Talcher, water from Brahmani river.

2. Cement Industry

  • Location Factors: Heavy and bulky raw materials like limestone, silica, gypsum, and coal. Requires high-quality limestone, hence located close to limestone deposits (Rajasthan, MP, Gujarat).

3. Electronics & IT Industry (Knowledge-based)

  • Characteristics: High reliance on human intelligence and skills rather than physical raw materials. Minimal environmental pollution.
  • Major Hub: Bengaluru (Silicon Valley of India) because of high concentration of engineering institutions, pleasant climate, supportive government policies, and excellent infrastructure.

ICSE Give-Reason Masterclass

Scenario / QuestionGeographical Reason
Why is Iron & Steel called a "Basic" or "Key" industry?Because all other industries depend on it for their machinery, transport equipment, tools, and structural materials.
Why are iron & steel plants located near coal fields or iron ore mines?The raw materials (iron ore, coal, limestone) are extremely heavy and bulky. Setting up plants nearby minimizes prohibitive transport costs.
Why is the sugar industry rapidly shifting from North India to South India?The tropical climate of South India yields sugarcane with higher sucrose content, and the maritime influence ensures a longer crushing season.
Why is the Jute industry concentrated along the Hooghly river?The river provides abundant clean, soft water necessary for retting (soaking) the jute stalks to strip the fibers.
Why is Bengaluru called the Silicon Valley of India?Because of its massive IT industry cluster, abundant skilled engineering professionals, pleasant year-round climate, and vast research institutions.
