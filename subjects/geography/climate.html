Factors Affecting Climate

(•‿•)
Artie is loading...

India's climate is described as Tropical Monsoon. Several geographic factors influence it:

Factor Impact on Indian Climate
Himalayas Act as a climatic divide. Protect India from cold central Asian winds and force monsoon winds to shed rain.
Latitude Tropic of Cancer divides India into tropical (south) and sub-tropical (north) zones.
Altitude Places at higher altitudes (like Shimla or Ooty) are cooler due to a decrease in temperature with height.
Distance from Sea Coastal areas experience an equable/maritime climate, while inland areas (like Delhi) experience extreme/continental climate.

The Four Seasons

(•‿•)
Artie is loading...

The Indian Meteorological Department recognizes four distinct seasons:

  • Hot Dry Season (March - May): Intense heating of the landmass. Hot local winds like Loo blow over northern plains. Mango Showers occur in South India.
  • South-West Monsoon (June - September): Moisture-laden winds strike the Malabar Coast first. Responsible for over 75% of India's rainfall. Divided into Arabian Sea branch and Bay of Bengal branch.
  • Retreating Monsoon (October - November): Transition period. Sun moves towards the southern hemisphere. High heat and humidity cause 'October Heat'. Brings rain to the Coromandel Coast.
  • Cold Season (December - February): Clear skies and low temperatures. Western Disturbances bring winter rain to Punjab/Haryana, crucial for Rabi crops.
