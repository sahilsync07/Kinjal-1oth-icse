Types of Farming

  • Subsistence Farming: Farming done primarily to feed the farmer's own family. Landholdings are small, old tools are used, and yields are low.
  • Commercial Farming: Farming done to sell crops in the market for profit. Large landholdings, modern machinery, HYV seeds, and fertilizers are used.
  • Plantation Farming: A type of commercial farming where a single crop (Tea, Coffee, Rubber) is grown on a large estate. Requires huge capital and cheap labour.

Food Crops

  • Rice (Kharif): Requires high temp (>24°C), heavy rain (>150 cm), and deep clayey/alluvial soil that retains water. Leading states: West Bengal, UP, Punjab.
  • Wheat (Rabi): Requires cool growing season (10-15°C), warm harvesting season (20-25°C), moderate rain (50-100 cm). Leading states: UP, Punjab, Haryana.
  • Millets (Jowar, Bajra, Ragi): Dry crops. Survive in high temp and low rain. Poor sandy/shallow black soil. Leading state: Rajasthan, Maharashtra.

Cash Crops

  • Sugarcane: Requires hot and humid climate (21-27°C), 75-100 cm rain. Takes almost a year to grow. UP and Maharashtra lead.
  • Cotton (Kharif): Needs 210 frost-free days, plenty of sunshine, and Black (Regur) soil. Gujarat and Maharashtra lead.
  • Jute (The Golden Fibre): Hot and humid climate, heavy rain (>200 cm), new alluvial soil. West Bengal leads.

Plantation Crops

  • Tea: Needs warm, moist, frost-free climate (20-30°C), heavy rainfall (150-300 cm) well distributed throughout the year, but stagnant water is fatal. Must be grown on hill slopes. Assam leads.
  • Coffee: Similar to tea but requires shade from direct sunlight. Grown on Western Ghats slopes (Karnataka leads).

ICSE Give-Reason Masterclass

Scenario / QuestionGeographical Reason
Why is Tea grown on hill slopes?Because stagnant water around the roots is fatal to the tea plant. Slopes ensure excellent drainage.
Why does Cotton require 210 frost-free days?Frost damages the cotton bolls and ruins the fibre quality.
Why is Jute called the Golden Fibre?Because of its shiny golden color and its high commercial value in making gunny bags and ropes.
Why must Sugarcane be crushed within 24 hours of harvesting?Because its sucrose content begins to dry up rapidly after cutting, reducing sugar yield.
Why are Millets considered the "poor man's food"?They are hardy crops that can be grown on infertile soil with very little rain, making them cheap to produce.
