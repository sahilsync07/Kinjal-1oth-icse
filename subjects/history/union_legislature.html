The Union Legislature

Welcome, Kinjal! Our first stop in Civics is understanding the Parliament of India. India has a bicameral legislature, meaning it has two houses: The Lok Sabha and the Rajya Sabha.

Module 1: The Lok Sabha

The Lok Sabha is the House of the People or the Lower House.

Feature Details
Composition Maximum strength is 550. Members are directly elected by the people.
Term 5 years, but it can be dissolved earlier by the President.
Qualifications Must be a citizen of India and at least 25 years of age.
Presiding Officer The Speaker, elected by the members from among themselves.

Module 2: The Rajya Sabha

(•‿•)

The Rajya Sabha is the Council of States or the Upper House.

  • Composition: Maximum strength is 250 (238 elected by State Assemblies + 12 nominated by the President).
  • Term: It is a permanent body and cannot be dissolved. Each member serves for 6 years, and one-third of the members retire every 2 years.
  • Qualifications: Must be a citizen of India and at least 30 years of age.
  • Presiding Officer: The Vice-President of India is the ex-officio Chairman.
