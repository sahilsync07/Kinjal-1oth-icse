World Wars & Aggressive Dictatorships

(•‿•)
Loading History-Bot...

1. World War I (1914–1918) & Versailles

  • **Causes**: Imperialist rivalries, division of Europe into hostile camps (Triple Alliance vs. Triple Entente), armament race, and the immediate trigger: the **Sarajevo assassination** of Archduke Franz Ferdinand.
  • **Treaty of Versailles (1919)**: A highly humiliating treaty imposed on Germany. Germany was forced to accept **complete War Guilt**, pay massive war reparations ($33 billion), surrender all colonies, and drastically **demilitarize its army** (limited to 100,000 soldiers, no air force, and demilitarization of the Rhineland).

2. Rise of Dictatorships: Fascism & Nazism

The humiliation of Versailles and the Great Depression paved the way for totalitarian regimes:

  • **Fascism (Italy under Benito Mussolini)**: Characterized by extreme nationalism, anti-communism, state worship, and aggressive imperial expansion (e.g. invasion of Abyssinia).
  • **Nazism (Germany under Adolf Hitler)**: Based on extreme racial superiority theories (Aryan race supremacy), violent anti-Semitism, rejection of the Versailles Treaty, and aggressive territorial expansion (*Lebensraum*).

3. World War II (1939–1945) Triggers

  • **Causes**: Humiliation of Germany under Versailles, failure of the League of Nations to stop aggression, Britain's policy of **appeasement** toward Hitler, and the immediate trigger: **Hitler's invasion of Poland (1 September 1939)**.

The United Nations & Non-Aligned Movement

(•‿•)
Loading History-Bot...

1. The United Nations Organization (UNO)

Founded on **24 October 1945** to maintain international peace and prevent future world wars.

  • **General Assembly**: The main deliberative body where every member nation has one vote.
  • **Security Council**: Responsible for maintaining peace. Consists of 15 members, including **5 permanent members** with absolute **Veto power** (US, UK, Russia, China, France).
  • **International Court of Justice (ICJ)**: The principal judicial organ of the UN, located at The Hague (Netherlands).
  • **Specialized Agencies**: **UNICEF** (children welfare), **UNESCO** (educational and cultural heritage), and **WHO** (global health services).

2. The Non-Aligned Movement (NAM)

Formed during the Cold War by newly independent nations who refused to join either the US-led Western bloc or the Soviet-led Eastern bloc.

  • **Founding Fathers**: Jawaharlal Nehru (India), Josip Broz Tito (Yugoslavia), Gamal Abdel Nasser (Egypt), Sukarno (Indonesia), and Kwame Nkrumah (Ghana).
  • **Panchsheel Principles**: The five principles of peaceful co-existence: mutual respect for territorial integrity, non-aggression, non-interference in internal affairs, equality, and peaceful co-existence.
