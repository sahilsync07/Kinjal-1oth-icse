Bose, Resignation & The Forward Bloc

(•‿•)

1. The Tripuri Crisis & Resignation (1939)

  • Ideological Differences: Bose wanted a radical, immediate struggle, taking advantage of Britain's involvement in WWII. Gandhi advocated for a cautious, non-violent approach and did not want to exploit Britain's war crisis.
  • Presidential Election: At the 1939 Tripuri Session, Bose contested the presidential election and **defeated Gandhi's candidate, Pattabhi Sitaramayya**. Gandhi declared it as *"my own defeat"*.
  • Resignation: Facing severe non-cooperation from the Congress Working Committee (who were loyal to Gandhi), Bose resigned from the presidency in April 1939. Dr. Rajendra Prasad was elected in his place.

2. Formation & Objectives of the Forward Bloc (1939)

After resigning, Bose founded the **Forward Bloc** as a left-wing faction within the Congress to rally radical elements. Its key objectives were:

  • **Liberation of India**: Complete and immediate independence with the support of all radical and anti-imperialist forces.
  • **Socialist Reconstruction**: Post-independence reconstruction of India on socialist lines, including modern industrial planning, abolition of landlordism, and a single national currency.

The Indian National Army (INA) & Azad Hind

(•‿•)
Loading History-Bot...

1. Origin and Founding of the INA

  • Captain Mohan Singh: An officer in the British Indian Army in Malaya. When the British retreated during WWII, Indian soldiers fell as prisoners of war (POWs) to the Japanese. Mohan Singh conceived the idea of using these POWs to fight for India's freedom, founding the **first INA** in 1942.
  • Rash Behari Bose: Organized the Indian Independence League in Tokyo and convened meetings to coordinate the INA. In 1943, he invited Subhas Chandra Bose to Singapore and handed over the leadership of the League and INA.

2. Netaji's Leadership & Azad Hind Government

  • Azad Hind Government: Formed in Singapore in October 1943. It was recognized by Axis powers (Germany, Japan, Italy).
  • Slogans and Campaigns: Bose gave the slogans **"Delhi Chalo"** and **"Jai Hind"**. He famously rallied the masses: *"Give me blood, and I will give you freedom!"*.
  • Rani of Jhansi Regiment: An all-women's combat unit commanded by **Captain Lakshmi Swaminathan (Sahgal)**, representing pioneering gender empowerment in armed struggles.

3. Battles & Historic Legacy

The INA fought alongside Japanese forces in Burma, capturing the keys to the eastern gateway (Moirang, Manipur). However, with the defeat of Japan in WWII and Netaji's mysterious disappearance in 1945, the INA campaign collapsed. The subsequent **Red Fort Trials** of INA officers (Sehgal, Dhillon, Shahnawaz) triggered nationwide military mutinies, proving to the British that they could no longer rely on the loyalty of the Indian armed forces.
