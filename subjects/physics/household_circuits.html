BOARD CRITICAL *Fuse & Switch Rule:* A fuse or a switch must **always** be connected in the **Live Wire**. If connected in the neutral wire, the appliance will remain connected to the high-voltage live wire ($220\text{ V}$) even when the fuse melts or the switch is turned OFF, creating a severe electric shock hazard.

1. Power Path to Home

Electricity is generated at $11\text{ kV}$, stepped up to $132\text{ kV}$ for long-distance transmission (reducing current and $I^2R$ power loss), and stepped down to $220\text{ V}$ before entering our homes through three wires: **Live (Phase)** at 220 V, **Neutral** at 0 V, and **Earth** at 0 V.

2. The Ring System (Modern Household Wiring)

An electrical ring circuit starts from the main distribution board, runs around all rooms, and returns to the board, forming a closed ring.
Advantages of Ring over traditional Tree system:

  1. Every appliance has two paths to the supply, so current divides. This allows **thinner, cheaper wires** to be used.
  2. Each appliance has its own individual fuse, so a fault in one does not shut down others.
  3. Plugs can be inserted anywhere in the ring.

Safety Devices: Fuses, Grounding & Plugs

(•‿•)

1. Electric Fuse

An electric fuse is a safety device which is used to limit the current in an electric circuit, preventing damage due to short circuits or overloading.
Fuse wire properties: Made of **Lead-Tin alloy** ($50\%\text{ Pb}, 50\%\text{ Sn}$). It has **high resistance** and a **low melting point** ($250^\circ\text{C}$). If current exceeds the rated limit, the wire heats up, melts, and breaks the circuit.

2. Miniature Circuit Breaker (MCB)

A modern automatic electromagnetic switch that switches off the circuit in less than $25\text{ milliseconds}$ when current exceeds safety limits. Unlike a fuse, it can be reset without replacing wires.

3. Earthing & Grounding

Connecting the metallic body of an appliance to the Earth using a thick copper wire. If a live wire touches the metal casing due to wear, current flows directly to Earth (path of zero resistance), causing the fuse in the live wire to blow immediately. This protects users from severe shocks.

4. The Three-Pin Plug Safety

  • The **Earth Pin** (top) is **longer and thicker** than the Live and Neutral pins.
    1. *Longer:* It enters the socket first, establishing the grounding path **before** the appliance gets connected to high-voltage live lines.
    2. *Thicker:* It cannot be accidentally inserted into the live or neutral socket holes.
