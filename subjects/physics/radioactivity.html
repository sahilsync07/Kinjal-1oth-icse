BOARD CRITICAL *Beta Decay Trap:* In a $\beta$-decay, a fast-moving electron is emitted from the **nucleus**, not the orbital shell! Inside an unstable nucleus, a neutron spontaneously converts into a proton and an electron:
₁⁰n → ₁¹p + ₀⁻¹e (beta particle) As a result, the atomic number ($Z$) increases by 1, while the mass number ($A$) remains completely unchanged.

2. Radioactive Decay Equations

  • Alpha (α) Decay:
    _Z^A X → _{Z-2}^{A-4} Y + ₂⁴He (or α) + Energy *Result:* Mass number ($A$) decreases by 4; Atomic number ($Z$) decreases by 2.
  • Beta (β) Decay:
    _Z^A X → _{Z+1}^A Y + _{-1}⁰e (or β) + Energy *Result:* Mass number ($A$) is unchanged; Atomic number ($Z$) increases by 1.
  • Gamma (γ) Emission:
    _Z^A X* → _Z^A X + γ (photon) *Result:* Excited nucleus releases energy; $A$ and $Z$ remain exactly the same.

1. Nuclear Fission

The splitting of a heavy nucleus into two lighter nuclei of nearly equal mass, accompanied by the release of neutrons and huge energy.

  • Example: Uranium-235 hit by a slow (thermal) neutron:
    ₉₂²³⁵U + ₀¹n → ₅₆¹⁴⁴Ba + ₃₆⁸⁹Kr + 3(₀¹n) + ~190 MeV Energy
  • Chain Reaction: The 3 released neutrons trigger fission in other U-235 atoms.
    1. *Controlled (Nuclear Reactor):* Excess neutrons are absorbed using **boron/cadmium control rods** and slowed down using **heavy water/graphite moderators**.
    2. *Uncontrolled (Atomic Bomb):* All neutrons cause cascade fission, releasing energy instantaneously.

2. Nuclear Fusion

The combining of two or more light nuclei to form a single heavier nucleus with massive energy release.

  • Example: Hydrogen isotopes combining:
    ₁²H + ₁³H → ₂⁴He + ₀¹n + 17.6 MeV Energy
  • Thermonuclear Reaction: Requires extremely high temperature ($\approx 10^7\text{ K}$) and pressure to overcome the electrostatic repulsion between positively charged hydrogen nuclei. This is the **energy source of the Sun and Stars**.

3. Safety & Background Radiation

  • Background Radiation: Radioactive rays present in the atmosphere at all times.
    1. *Internal (Natural):* Potassium-40, Carbon-14 inside food and tissues.
    2. *External (Natural/Artificial):* Cosmic rays, Radon gas, nuclear medical waste.
  • Safety Rules: Store waste in **thick lead casks** buried deep underground; use **robotic arms** and **lead-apron clothing**; monitor dosage with **film badges**.
